Before we meet

What to expect in your 30-minute consultation

No slide decks and no sales pressure. You'll talk directly with a senior engineer from our Frisco, TX team who works on projects like yours every week. We listen first, then give you a straight read on what's feasible, what it takes, and what we'd do next whether that's AI development, cloud work or IT staffing for your team in the United States.

Who you'll meet

A senior Techsara engineer or solutions lead not a sales rep. Someone who can answer architecture, delivery and staffing questions live on the call.

What we'll cover

Your goals and constraints, the current state of your stack and team, the options on the table, and a realistic view of timeline, risk and cost.

What you'll leave with

Clear next steps and, within one business day, a written follow-up with a tailored recommendation engagement model, scope and rough investment.

  • Bring whatever you have a brief, a rough idea, or just a problem to talk through.
  • An NDA is available on request before we meet.
